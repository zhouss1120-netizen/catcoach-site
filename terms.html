2. What CatCoach provides

CatCoach starts with a behavior category, a relevant health note and a short questionnaire. It suggests likely behavioral causes and previews a 14-day roadmap before you unlock the full plan. Cat profile editing is optional and does not interrupt the initial check. An unlocked plan provides daily task steps, completion records, check-ins, progress views and a round review on iPhone and iPad. Your first 14-day plan is built on your device from templates based on published feline behavior guidance. Later rounds can be adjusted by AI only if you allow it; otherwise they are adjusted on your device.
