A few useful answers.

How do purchases work?

CatCoach offers a weekly auto-renewing subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices and billing terms are shown in the app before purchase. A 14-day plan spans two weekly billing periods. Apple handles payment. See our subscription terms.

How do I restore or cancel?

Restore from the subscription screen or Settings in CatCoach, using the same Apple Account. Cancel a weekly subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For refunds, visit Apple’s refund page.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Open Settings → Erase All Local Data. You can also delete individual cat profiles or photos. Purchase records handled by Apple and RevenueCat are separate; contact us for purchase-related data requests.
