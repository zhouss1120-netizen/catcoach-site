A few useful answers.

How do purchases work?

Preview the assessment and roadmap before you buy. CatCoach Weekly is an auto-renewing subscription; CatCoach Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Both unlock the same plan features. Apple shows the local price and billing terms before confirmation. A 14-day plan is separate from the 1-week billing period. See our subscription terms.

How do I restore or cancel?

Restore from Settings → Restore Purchases in CatCoach, using the same Apple Account. Weekly members can use Settings → Manage Subscription, or Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For refunds, visit Apple’s refund page.

Will my cat’s records appear on another device?

Purchases can be restored with the same Apple Account. Cat profiles, photos, plans and records are stored locally and do not automatically sync between your iPhone and iPad.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Open CatCoach → Settings → Data & Privacy → Erase All Local Data. You can also delete individual cat profiles or photos. Purchase records handled by Apple and RevenueCat are separate; contact us for purchase-related data requests.
